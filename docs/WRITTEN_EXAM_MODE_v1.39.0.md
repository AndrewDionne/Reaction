# v1.39.0 — Written Exam Mode

## Purpose

Adds an end-of-year written exam builder for the Year 9 Science app. The mode is designed for exams that ask pupils to **state**, **identify**, **describe**, **explain** and **calculate** rather than only choose multiple-choice answers.

## Exam sizes

| Option | Total marks | Biology | Chemistry | Physics |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Quick | 15 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Standard | 30 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Full | 45 | 15 | 15 | 15 |

## Student workflow

1. Select **Written exam mode** from the learning hub.
2. Choose 15, 30 or 45 marks.
3. Write an answer in the text box.
4. Reveal the mark scheme.
5. Self-mark using the mark buttons.
6. Finish the exam and review Biology / Chemistry / Physics mark balance.

## Answer-format coaching

Each written question gives the pupil a short answer frame:

- **State / identify:** exact fact or term only.
- **Describe:** what happens or what a graph/diagram shows, using values where available.
- **Explain:** science reason using because / so / therefore.
- **Calculate:** formula, substitution and answer with units.

## Content design

The v1.39.0 bank starts with 18 high-value written questions: 6 Biology, 6 Chemistry and 6 Physics. The builder uses fixed blueprints so every paper is exactly balanced by science domain.
